## How I read the criterion
- **Missing part:** a missing `date`, `time` or `zone` key counts as "a part missing". The whole example then replaces the form, not just the missing field. For example, `?date=2026-11-02` shows the 2026-10-31 example. A missing `guests` key does not count, because the frozen R4.1 contract treats a link without guests as valid, with an empty list.
- **Empty values:** a part that is present but empty (`?date=`) is not missing. It shows the form as typed, with the date refusal beside it.
- **Link form:** "the `?date=...` form of 4.1" means `?date=…&time=…&zone=…&guests=a,b`, with the guests tidied (trimmed, empties dropped). The `pick` parameter is not carried over on submit.
- **`buildView` call:** the frozen R4.1 contract requires the literal `buildView(window.location.search)` in `main.js`. The example case therefore calls `buildView(form.search)` in one branch, and a complete link uses the literal call in the other.
