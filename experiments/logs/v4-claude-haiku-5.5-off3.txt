**What I built**
- **Task 3 (`src/resolve.js`):** `resolveLocal` turns a local date, time and zone into a UTC instant. It refuses skipped times with a sentence naming the range (for example `02:00 to 03:00`). For repeated times it returns an `ambiguous` result with both instants, and returns one instant when `earlier` or `later` is passed.
- **Task 4 (`src/guests.js`, `src/night.js`):** `planGuests` builds one row per guest with local date, `HH:MM`, offset, day label (including "2 days before/after"), and dead-of-night flag. Rows sort by local date and time, then zone name. A bad guest zone fails the whole plan with a sentence naming it.
- **Task 5 (`src/kinder.js`):** `suggestKinderTime` checks each hour of the host date. Skipped hours are never offered, repeated hours offer both instants, and ties go to the earliest hour.
